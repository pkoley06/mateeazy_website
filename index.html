Real screens from the app. Pick a feature to watch how it's done.

Find rooms and flatmates

  1. Open Discover. Rooms and flatmates near you show up first, each with your match %.

  2. Switch between All, Rooms and Flatmates, or tap the filter button to set rent, room type and distance.

  3. Tap the heart on a card to save it for later.

Look inside a room

  1. Tap any room card to open it.

  2. Swipe through the photos and check the rent, deposit and move-in date.

  3. See who you'd live with, how your habits fit, and where it is on the map.

Like and comment

  1. Open a room and scroll down to the likes and comments.

  2. Tap the heart to like it, or ask the host a question in the comments.

  3. The host gets a notification and can reply.

Chat in the app

  1. Open Chats to see every conversation. Search, or show only unread ones.

  2. Each chat shows the room you're talking about at the top.

  3. Send messages and photos without giving out your phone number.

Room Finder Mode

  1. Turn on Room Finder Mode and set the area and budget you're looking for.

  2. Open the Flatmates tab in Discover to see others searching near you.

  3. Open a profile, compare your everyday rhythm and tap Message to team up.

Set your everyday rhythm

  1. Go to Profile and tap a habit under Your everyday rhythm.

  2. Pick the answer that sounds most like you and tap Save & next.

  3. Your match % with every room and flatmate updates to match.

Post your room

  1. Go to Profile and tap List your room.

  2. Add photos, rent, deposit, move-in date, furnishing and house rules, and choose who can apply.

  3. Publish it. People nearby see it in Discover with their match %.

Notifications and privacy

  1. Go to Profile and open settings.

  2. Choose which notifications reach your phone: likes, messages, new seekers and new rooms.

  3. Manage blocked users, or disable or delete your account any time.

    Questions people ask

    Is MateEazy free?

    Yes. Downloading the app, posting a room, searching and chatting are all free, and there are no broker fees.

    Which cities does it work in?

    MateEazy works anywhere in India. Search is based on where you are and the radius you choose, not a fixed list of cities. We've just launched, so listings are still growing. Posting your room or turning on Room Finder Mode helps people near you find you.

    How is the match score calculated?

    We compare your answers on cleanliness, sleep timing, guests and food. Cleanliness counts the most, then sleep, guests and food. If one of you doesn't smoke and the other smokes regularly, it's treated as a dealbreaker and the score is 0%.

    Can I use it without having a room?

    Yes. Browse rooms near you, or turn on Room Finder Mode so hosts and other seekers can find you and team up to rent a place together.

    Is there an iPhone app?

    Not yet. MateEazy is on Android right now.

    Who can see my phone number?

    Nobody, unless you share it. All conversations happen in the in-app chat.

    How do I delete my account?

    Open Settings in the app and choose Delete account. You can also read how account deletion works.

    How do I contact you?

    Email support@mateeazy.in and we'll get back to you.
